# Code of Conduct

To foster a welcoming and respectful community, all participants in the spriteself project are expected to adhere to the following guidelines:

- Be respectful and considerate in all communications.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or offensive behavior.
- Respect differing opinions and constructive feedback.
- Use inclusive language.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by contacting the project maintainer privately at:

arthurrabelo@gmail.com

Reports will be handled confidentially and promptly to ensure a safe environment for everyone.

Thank you for helping us maintain a positive community atmosphere.
